## How These Connect to the Main Arc

These three videos form a **hardware + AI sub-arc** within PewDiePie's broader "tech fencing" journey:

1. **Linux Video** → Takes control of his OS
2. **de-Google Video** → Takes control of his services/ecosystem
3. **Accidentally Built a Nuclear Supercomputer** → Takes control of his compute (builds the hardware)
4. **STOP. Using AI Right now** → Takes control of his AI (self-hosts models, builds ChatOS)
5. **I Trained My Own AI** → Takes control of model quality (fine-tunes his own)
6. **I Fixed YouTube** (Mar 17, 2026) → **Applies** everything he built to fix his daily experience

The "I Fixed YouTube" video is where he coins the term **"tech fencing"** and uses his self-hosted AI to create a browser extension that removes Shorts, suggested videos, and sidebar clutter from YouTube. He also mentions the existing tool **Unhook** as an alternative for people who don't want to build their own AI.

### Key Insight for [TechFencing.com](http://TechFencing.com)

These secondary videos are less about *teaching* specific tools and more about the **philosophy and appliance**: if you own your own compute, you can build your own tools. The AI he trained on his supercomputer literally produced the extension that "fixed" YouTube. That's the full circle of tech fencing.
